Let's Write Together

one shared document. one word a minute. everyone gets a say

Every minute, the crowd adds one word to today's document.

  • Click the red box to join the current round.
  • In the first 30 seconds of each minute, you can type and submit one word. Each player can submit one word per minute. Submitting votes for that word.
  • For the whole minute, you can vote for any submitted word, including your own.
  • Click another word to move your vote. Click the word you voted for to remove it. You can change your vote until the minute ends.
  • When the minute ends, the word with the most votes is added to the document. If two words have the same number of votes, the earliest submission wins.
  • If no word was submitted or nobody voted, the round is skipped and nothing is added.
